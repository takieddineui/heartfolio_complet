Fonts bundled here are DejaVu fonts (https://dejavu-fonts.github.io),
derived from Bitstream Vera, licensed under a permissive free license
that explicitly allows embedding/redistribution in commercial products.
See https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html for the full text.

Bundled deliberately (rather than relying on whatever fonts happen to
be installed on a given OS/container) so rendering is identical in
development and production regardless of deployment image — see the
"Fonts" note in apps/delivery/renderers/love_story_timeline/png_renderer.py.
