Fonts bundled here:

- DejaVu fonts (https://dejavu-fonts.github.io), version 2.37, derived
  from Bitstream Vera, licensed under a permissive free license that
  explicitly allows embedding/redistribution in commercial products. Full
  text in LICENSE-dejavu.txt (the 2.37 release's own LICENSE file).
- Noto Naskh Arabic and Noto Kufi Arabic (noto-*-arabic.woff2), the same
  files the site serves from frontend-heartfolio/public/fonts, under the
  SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full text in LICENSE-noto-arabic.txt.

Bundled deliberately (rather than relying on whatever fonts happen to
be installed on a given OS/container) so rendering is identical in
development and production regardless of deployment image — see the
"Fonts" note in apps/delivery/renderers/love_story_timeline/png_renderer.py.
