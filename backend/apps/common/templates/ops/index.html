Ops

Staff tools. Same login as the Django admin.

Reports Revenue, packs, countries, and the feedback customers have left. Lookup Find an order by email or partial ID. Read-only — shows what happened. Re-send Send a paid order's receipt again, with a fresh 30-day link. Reconcile An order that was paid but never delivered — ask Paddle and settle it. Status Is the database reachable, is storage configured, is Paddle answering.
