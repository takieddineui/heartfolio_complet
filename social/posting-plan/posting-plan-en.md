# Heartfolio — Posting plan (English)

One caption + hashtag set per piece. Captions use **honest claims only**:
keepsakes are delivered instantly as a private link and a QR card; Love Story
Timeline and Song Poster also include print‑ready files; **nothing is shipped
or printed for you**. Example lines are drawn from our own sample content and
published guides and are clearly labelled as examples — never customer quotes.

Core hashtag bank (mix 8–12 per post, trim for the platform):
`#Heartfolio #personalizedgift #digitalkeepsake #giftideas #meaningfulgifts
#keepsake #QRgift #personalisedgifts #thoughtfulgifts`
