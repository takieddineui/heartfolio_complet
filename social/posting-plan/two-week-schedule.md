# Heartfolio — First two weeks (suggested order)

The plan **alternates the two series day by day**: Series A (printable posters)
and Series B (the live/digital experience). It mixes formats (single post,
carousel, reel) and all four products, and opens with the explainer carousel so
new followers understand what Heartfolio is.

Every piece exists in **English, French and Arabic** (`series-*/<lang>/…`). Use
the matching‑language caption from `posting-plan-<lang>.md`. Based on order
history to date (far more French than English, no Arabic yet), a sensible launch
is **French + English as primary feeds, Arabic ready to switch on** with a
native review of the captions first.

Cadence: one feed piece per day (reels perform well mid‑week and on weekends).
Captions, hashtags and the honest‑claims note are in the per‑language files.

## Honest‑claims reminder (applies to every caption)

Delivered instantly as a **private link + QR card**; **no app, no account**.
Love Story Timeline and Song Poster also include **print‑ready files** — but
**nothing is shipped or printed for you**. Song Poster plays the track **from
YouTube** (we don't sell or host music; the soundwave is designed per order, not
extracted from the recording). Example lines are labelled as examples, never
customer testimonials — and we never invent names, reviews, ratings or numbers.
